Safety Recommendations

Driving safety tips based on personal habits and general crash patterns

⚠️

Reduce Speeding

Speeding increases crash severity and reduces reaction time. Keep speed within posted limits, especially in rain or at night.

High Priority
🚗

Maintain Safe Distance

Rear-end collisions are common when drivers follow too closely. Keep enough space between your vehicle and the vehicle ahead.

Medium Priority
✅

Avoid Harsh Braking

Harsh braking can indicate distracted driving, speeding, or poor following distance. Brake smoothly whenever possible.

Medium Priority

Recommended Driving Habits

1

Slow down during bad weather and wet road conditions.

2

Avoid unnecessary night driving when tired.

3

Use seatbelts for every trip, even short trips.

4

Keep both hands on the wheel and avoid phone use while driving.

5

Brake smoothly and maintain a safe following distance.

How Recommendations Are Generated

The app compares user driving behavior with historical crash data. Based on patterns such as speed, road condition, weather, time of day, and collision type, the system suggests safer driving actions.

User Data

Speeding, braking, acceleration, trip time, and trip frequency.

Historical Data

Crash type, weather, road condition, injury severity, and recovery outcome.

AI Insight

Matches risky patterns and recommends safer driving habits.
